## 7. Router, Wireless Extender & Fixed Voice Troubleshooting FAQs

### A. Router & Wi-Fi Troubleshooting
1. Check whether Wi-Fi is turned on on your device (phone/laptop) and router power is ON.
2. Check whether all power, Ethernet, and telephone/Fibre cables are tightly connected.
3. Check whether the router **WLAN** LED is ON (if OFF, press the **Wi-Fi / WLAN button** on the router).
4. Restart the router, change Wi-Fi password if needed, and check quota usage via the **MySLT App**.

### B. Wireless Extender Setup (WPS)
1. Plug the wireless extender into a wall outlet.
2. Press the **WPS button** on the router.
3. Press the **WPS button** on the wireless extender.

### C. ONT LED Status Indicators
* **`Phone` LED OFF:** Unable to register Voice account — call **`1212`** (Note: for a single voice connection, `Phone 2` is always OFF).
* **`Phone` LED Solid / Flashing:** Voice account registered / call in progress.
* **`WLAN` LED ON / Flashing:** Wi-Fi interface started / transmitting data (`OFF` = press Wi-Fi button).
* **`GPON` LED Solid Green:** Optical link between ONT and OLT is operating normally.

### D. Fixed Voice / Landphone Troubleshooting
* **Cannot hear dial tone / Noise or disturbance on line:** Check whether telephone cables are tightly connected.
* **Ringing sound is very low:** Adjust the ring tone volume button on the telephone unit to **High**.
* **Phone wire inside or outside premises is damaged:** Log a complaint via **`1212`** or the **MySLT App** (`https://myslt.slt.lk/`).
