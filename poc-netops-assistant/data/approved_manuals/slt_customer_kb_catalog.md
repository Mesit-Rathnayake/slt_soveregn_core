## Section 5: Self-Care USSD Portals, Loans & Account Balances
1. #170# Mobile Data & Self-Care USSD Portal:
   - Check Data Remaining: Dial #170# or open MySLT App -> Internet -> Data Remaining (https://myslt.slt.lk/).
   - Activate Data Loan: Dial #170# -> Option 8 ("Data Loan"), or dial directly: #170#36# (Rs. 36 - 296 MB, 3 days), #170#58# (Rs. 58 - 512 MB, 7 days), #170#118# (Rs. 118 - 1.1 GB, 14 days), #170#159# (Rs. 159 - 1.5 GB, 14 days).
   - Check Data Loan Balance: SMS DB to 7678 or dial #170#.
   - Loan Repayment: Automatically recovered on next mobile recharge.

2. #247# Instant Credit Loan USSD Portal:
   - Usable for local outgoing calls, local SMS, internet browsing, and Value-Added Services (VAS).
   - Rs. 25 Loan: Dial #247#25# (Service charge: Rs. 1.75 + taxes | Validity: 5 days | Age on network > 3 months).
   - Rs. 40 Loan: Dial #247# on Tue / Wed / Thu (Service charge: Rs. 3.00 + taxes | Validity: 7 days | Age on network > 3 months).
   - Rs. 50 Loan: Dial #247#50# (Service charge: Rs. 3.50 + taxes | Validity: 7 days | Age on network > 3 months).
   - Rs. 75 Loan: Dial #247#75# (Service charge: Rs. 5.25 + taxes | Validity: 7 days | Age on network > 3 months).
   - Rs. 90 Loan: Dial #247# on Fri / Sat / Sun / Mon (Service charge: Rs. 6.00 + taxes | Validity: 7 days | Age on network > 3 months).
   - Rs. 100 Loan: Dial #247#100# (Service charge: Rs. 7.00 + taxes | Validity: 7 days | Age on network > 6 months).
   - Repayment: Automatically recovered on next mobile recharge.

3. #1456# Billing & Usage Information Portal (and 1456 IVR):
   - Current usage, account balance, last month bill, total billed usage, last payment, billed date, billing period, and credit limit.

4. #888# Self-Care Information Portal:

## Section 6: SIM, eSIM, APN, mCash, Roaming & New Connections
- eSIM Compatibility: Dial *#06# on phone and confirm EID number is displayed (https://sltmobitel.lk/esim).
- Mobile Data APN: Set APN to 'mobitel'.
- Credit Limit Alert: SMS notification sent at 75% usage of credit limit.
- Required Documents for New Connection:
  1. Completed application form.
  2. Identity proof: National Identity Card (NIC), Driving License, or Passport.
  3. Valid billing proof: Water bill, electricity bill, fixed phone bill, credit card statement, or current account statement.
- mCash (#111#):
  - Dial #111# on mobile.
  - Remove saved bank account: mCash Main Menu -> Top Up Via Bank Account -> select bank account -> Delete icon -> Confirm.
- International Roaming & IDD (#999#):
  - Dial #999# on mobile to activate or manage roaming data plans (https://sltmobitel.lk/personal/idd-and-roaming).

## Section 7: Router, Wireless Extender & Technical Troubleshooting FAQs
1. Router & Wi-Fi Troubleshooting:
   - Check whether Wi-Fi is enabled on device and router power is ON.
   - Check whether power, Ethernet, and telephone/Fibre cables are tightly connected.
   - Check whether WLAN LED is ON (press Wi-Fi / WLAN button if OFF) and GPON LED is solid green.
   - Restart router, change Wi-Fi password if needed, and check data quota usage in MySLT App.

2. Slow Internet Troubleshooting:
   - Check data quota: Check remaining quota via MySLT App / Portal (https://myslt.slt.lk/).
   - Restart router.
   - Change Wi-Fi password to disconnect unauthorized devices.
   - Check telephone/Fibre cable connection.

3. Wireless Extender Setup (WPS):
   - Step 1: Plug wireless extender into wall outlet.
   - Step 2: Press WPS button on router.
   - Step 3: Press WPS button on wireless extender.

4. ONT LED Indicators:
   - Phone LED OFF: Voice account unallocated/unregistered - call 1212.
   - WLAN LED ON/Flashing: Wi-Fi active (press Wi-Fi button if OFF).
   - GPON LED Solid Green: Optical link to OLT operating normally.

5. Fixed Voice / Landphone Troubleshooting:
   - Noise or no dial tone: Check telephone cables are tightly connected.
   - Ring volume low: Adjust ring volume button on telephone to High.
   - Damaged wire: Log complaint via 1212 or MySLT App.
